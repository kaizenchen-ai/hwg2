🗣️ 課本句型回顧

📌 家人句型：
• A: Who's he? B: He's my father.
• A: Who's she? B: She's my mother.
• He's tall. / She's short.
📌 職業句型：
• A: Are you a teacher? B: Yes, I am.
• A: Is he a doctor? B: No, he's not. He's a nurse.
• A: Is she a driver? B: No, she's not. She's a cook.
